Who we are

Peflo Logistics Limited is a construction company. We build and renovate buildings, carry out civil and external works, and manage and supervise construction projects.

We also organise materials, equipment and deliveries, so that work on site is not held up waiting for supplies.

We work to applicable laws, building codes, permits and safety requirements, and we agree scope, price and programme in writing before work starts.
